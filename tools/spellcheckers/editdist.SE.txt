## Transition pair specifications + weight:

## giella-core 1.16.12 and later), so a direction that must stay at
## DEFAULT_WEIGHT is listed explicitly at 10.

## typo corpus, and ŋ -> ñ would license suggestions spelled with ñ, which is
## not part of the orthography. ñ -> ŋ stays at 0 - that is normalisation of
## a substitute letter, not a typo.
